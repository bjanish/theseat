# Feature Ideas — The Seat

## Approved (Post-Ship or Next Sprint)

### Question Count Badge on Join Button
When someone's on the solo screen deciding whether to join, show "3 questions waiting" on the Join button. Social proof that the session is active and lively — pulls people in faster.

### "Skipped" Feedback for Players
When the host swipes past a question without answering, the player who asked it currently has no idea. Show a subtle "Skipped" state (replacing "Your question is in") to close the feedback loop without revealing who asked what.

### Anonymous Reactions
After the host answers out loud, players tap a reaction (fire, skull, crying-laughing) that appears briefly on the host's screen. Zero text, one tap, fully anonymous. Keeps energy high between questions without breaking anonymity.

## Considered but Deferred

### Multiple Questions Per Player Per Host
Currently one question per host — locked after sending, resets on seat pass. Idea: unlock after the host answers (taps to dismiss full-screen question) so players can send follow-ups without requiring a seat pass.

### Shuffle on Pass (REJECTED)
Carry unanswered questions to the new host's queue. Rejected because questions are personalized — "What's the wildest thing YOU'VE done?" doesn't make sense directed at a different person.
