# Marketing Notes — The Seat

## Taglines / Copy Ideas

- "Play with friends on a camping trip. No WiFi needed."

- "No internet. No cellular. Everyone plays on their own phone."

## Landing Page Insight (Aug 24)

- Lead with the social experience, not the tech. The sell is the questions and the room energy — not peer-to-peer networking.
- "Your friends will ask you things they'd never say out loud." — hero tagline
- Show actual provocative questions on the landing page in Cinzel/gold card style (mirrors the in-app host screen). The page IS the preview of the experience.
- Networking (no WiFi, no internet, connects directly) is a supporting detail, not the headline. Mention it, don't lead with it.
- Hook quote: "I can't believe someone actually asked that." — Every group that plays.
- The anonymity is what sells it. "Nobody knows who asked. That's the point."
- Structure: questions first → social hook → how it works → features (incl. tech) → download
